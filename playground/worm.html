C. elegans connectome

Worm brain simulator. The complete nervous system of the nematode C. elegans, mapped synapse by synapse from electron micrographs: 302 neurons and about 5,700 connections, running live and driving a virtual worm.

Doing- Firing- Senses-

Smells reach AWC, AWA and ASE. Heat and cold reach AFD, ASH, FLP and PVD, touch reaches ALM, AVM and PLM, and the arena walls press on the nose. Crawling, backing up and turning come out of the network; finding food gets a small built-in nudge.

Keys: 1–4 tools, H controls, A activity, C connectivity.

Connectome data: Cook et al. 2019 (from the White et al. 1986 electron micrographs), via the OpenWorm c302 project, MIT licence. Synapse signs: Fenyves et al. 2020, CC BY 4.0.
