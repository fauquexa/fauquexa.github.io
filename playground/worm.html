Hector the worm

This is the actual mapped-out nervous system of the worm C. elegans: 302 neurons and about 5,700 connections between them and the muscles, wired into a virtual body. Hector is essentially living a little virtual life on this website.

Doing- Firing- Senses-

Smells reach the chemosensory neurons (AWC, AWA, ASE), heat and cold reach AFD, ASH, FLP and PVD, touch reaches ALM, AVM and PLM, and bumping the dish wall presses the nose neurons. The motor neurons drive the body wall muscles. Whether he crawls forward, backs up or turns comes out of the network; homing in on food also gets a small built-in nudge.

Connectome data: Cook et al. 2019 (from the White et al. 1986 electron micrographs), via the OpenWorm c302 project, MIT licence. Synapse signs: Fenyves et al. 2020, CC BY 4.0.
