Hector the worm

Hector runs on the real, fully mapped nervous system of the worm C. elegans: 302 neurons and about 5,700 connections, wired into a little virtual body.

Doing- Firing- Senses-

Smells reach AWC, AWA and ASE. Heat and cold reach AFD, ASH, FLP and PVD, touch reaches ALM, AVM and PLM, and the dish wall presses his nose. Crawling, backing up and turning come out of the network; finding food gets a small built-in nudge.

Connectome data: Cook et al. 2019 (from the White et al. 1986 electron micrographs), via the OpenWorm c302 project, MIT licence. Synapse signs: Fenyves et al. 2020, CC BY 4.0.
