I picked a slime mould. Physarum polycephalum has no brain and no neurons, yet it finds short paths between food, and in 2010 one grew a network strikingly close to the Tokyo rail system. Here, tens of thousands of particles that can only smell three spots just ahead of them do the same. Put oat flakes down and watch it connect them.
